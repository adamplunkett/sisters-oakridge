Where the numbers come from

Everything solid is the route file itself. Our four days are a subsection of Three Sisters Three Rivers, a published 325-mile route whose GPX has 21,336 track points carrying distance and elevation. Sisters to Oakridge is 6,301 of them: 134.5 miles, +16,374 / −18,327 ft. Distance, grade and climbing here are read straight off that file rather than inferred.

It confirms the itinerary, with two exceptions. Every waypoint agrees to within half a mile and about twenty feet. But the original Day 4 is given as 14.2 mi / +1,633 ft, and the file makes it 18.0 mi / +2,244 ft — nearly four miles and six hundred feet light. And the high point after Cougar is 5,018 ft at mile 93.2, not 4,967 at 92.8.

One segment is still an estimate: the Larison Rock road climb, drawn dashed, because it is not on the parent route at all. Larison Rock's descent is OpenStreetMap geometry with USGS elevations.

This is not a navigation track. Carry the real GPX.
